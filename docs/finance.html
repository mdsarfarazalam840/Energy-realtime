        SEC EDGAR deep-dive panel

        Read the panel

        • Snapshot totals the tracked filers, filings, reported revenue, and average net margin.
        • Filing stream lists the newest 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K documents. Click a row to open the filer.
        • Sector exposure ranks sectors by reported revenue. Click a sector to filter the board.

        Deep dive

        • The focus panel shows the latest annual facts: revenue, net income, margin, ROE, assets, equity, EPS, and operating cash flow.
        • The chart compares revenue and net income. Switch between annual and quarterly periods.
        • Every filing link opens the primary document on sec.gov in a new tab.

        Data path

        www.sec.gov + data.sec.gov → export_edgar_data.py → docs/data/finance.json → this page.

        The exporter identifies itself with a SEC User-Agent, throttles below 10 requests per second, and stores only annual and recent quarterly facts. Missing facts show a dash instead of a guess.
